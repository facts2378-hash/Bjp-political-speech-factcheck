Sukanta Majumdar · 26 March 2025

Claim: Saraswati Puja was not allowed at Jadavpur University

FALSE

Sukanta Majumdar claimed that Saraswati Puja was not allowed at Jadavpur University while Iftar was permitted.

Assessment: The claim was found to be false by fact-checking based on available evidence concerning Saraswati Puja at the university.

Evidence: Records and reports concerning the organisation of Saraswati Puja at Jadavpur University.

Fact-check source: Alt News

Last checked: 29 August 2026

Rajeev Chandrasekhar · 2023

Claim about a Bengaluru murder

FALSE

Rajeev Chandrasekhar made a claim describing the victim of a Bengaluru murder as a Hindu seer and suggested a communal angle.

Assessment: Fact-checking found the description of the victim and the claimed communal angle to be inaccurate.

Evidence: Police statements and independent verification of the circumstances were examined.

Anurag Thakur · January 2020

Speech involving the "goli maaro" slogan

HATE-SPEECH RELATED

During a Delhi election campaign event, remarks by Anurag Thakur were followed by a crowd chant involving the "goli maaro" slogan.

Assessment: The Election Commission found the remarks objectionable and held that they could promote enmity between different religious communities.

Important: This records the reported Election Commission finding. It does not state that a criminal court convicted the speaker of a hate-speech offence.

Source: BOOM / Election Commission reporting

Last checked: 29 August 2026

Our Methodology

We aim to evaluate factual claims using consistent evidence-based standards.

  • Identify the exact claim being made.
  • Determine the date, context and definition relevant to the claim.
  • Prefer primary and authoritative sources whenever available.
  • Compare the claim with the evidence.
  • Explain uncertainty when evidence is incomplete or disputed.

हमारी तथ्य-जांच पद्धति

हमारा उद्देश्य लगातार और सबूत-आधारित मानकों के अनुसार तथ्यात्मक दावों का मूल्यांकन करना है।

  • किए गए सटीक दावे की पहचान करना।
  • दावे की तारीख, संदर्भ और परिभाषा निर्धारित करना।
  • जहां संभव हो, प्राथमिक और आधिकारिक स्रोतों को प्राथमिकता देना।
  • दावे की तुलना उपलब्ध सबूतों से करना।
  • सबूत अधूरे या विवादित होने पर अनिश्चितता स्पष्ट करना।
